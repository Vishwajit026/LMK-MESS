3. REAL-TIME CHAT ARCHITECTURE & SOCKET EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. `joinRoom`         : Join channel, load history & broadcast presence
2. `leaveRoom`        : Leave channel & notify members
3. `chatMessage`      : Sub-millisecond room broadcast + persistent MongoDB storage
4. `typing`           : Dynamic "User is typing..." indicator with debounced lifecycle
5. `onlineUsersUpdate`: Live presence tracking per room and server-wide
6. `messageReaction`  : Instant emoji reaction sync (👍, ❤️, 😂, 🔥, 🚀, 👏)
7. `directMessage`    : Private 1-on-1 direct messaging channels
